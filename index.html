About us

QuantTech Company is built by practitioners. Our work draws on years of buy-side quantitative research owning end-to-end systematic macro strategies across equity-index, bond, and commodity futures and FX forwards — the full lifecycle of signal development, data engineering, backtesting, portfolio construction, and risk attribution.

Our mission is simple: make institutional-grade, point-in-time systematic-macro data, factors, and research available as a reliable subscription input — so any systematic firm can research and validate strategies on a leakage-safe foundation without building a data factory from scratch.

We operate as an independent research and data vendor — we do not trade, manage capital, or give investment advice. That keeps our incentives aligned with our clients and our position clean: we deliver data, models, and tooling; clients make and execute every investment decision themselves.

The edge in modern systematic investing is no longer the model alone — it is the speed and integrity of the data feeding it, and the rigor of the methods applied to it. That is where we compete.

The infrastructure we build to run our own research is the product.

Our approach

Most CTA and GTAA offerings still rely on simple linear, rules-based signals. We apply modern machine learning and deep learning to extract richer, non-linear structure from market and alternative data — disciplined at every step by financial-time-series-aware validation.

Non-linear signal extraction

Gradient-boosted trees, random forests, and deep-learning architectures applied to liquid futures and alternative data, with domain-specific feature engineering — surfacing predictive structure that linear models miss. Where incumbents stop at linear factors, we begin.

Validation-first by construction

ML and AI accelerate hypothesis generation — and amplify overfitting. Every model passes the same leakage-safe gates we apply internally: combinatorial purged cross-validation, the Deflated Sharpe Ratio, stationary block bootstrap, and human-in-the-loop review. Powerful methods, honestly tested.
